## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-05

First public release of CherryTools, a menu bar utility for Apple silicon Macs.

- **Ports:** See listening ports, whether each one is local or reachable from the network, and CPU, memory, and power use. Search, filter, and terminate processes.
- **Clipboard:** Keep text and images for 30 days, with search, keyboard selection, pause, and app exclusions. History is off until you turn it on, stays on your Mac, and skips items that apps mark as private.
- **Finder cut:** Use ⌘X and ⌘V to move files, or **Cut with CherryTools** and **Move Cut Files Here** in Finder's right-click menu.
- **Toolbox:** Flush the DNS cache with one click.
- **Accessibility:** Text and controls meet WCAG AA contrast in light, dark, and Increase Contrast appearances, with VoiceOver labels and keyboard navigation.
